Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Aivenor is a personal research tool for reviewing publicly available marketplace listings and Pinterest Pins related to print-on-demand products. This policy describes how the application handles information when it is used by its operator.

Information the application accesses

  • Public listing or Pin information returned by the connected platform, such as titles, descriptions, image links, creation dates, save counts when provided, and links to the original page.
  • Basic Pinterest account information returned during authorization, such as the authorized account identifier or username.
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens needed to keep the operator's Pinterest connection active.
  • Search terms, filters, notes, and usage marks entered by the operator in the local research library.

How information is used

The application uses this information to search, score, deduplicate, and display research results for the operator's product-selection workflow. It does not create Pins, publish content, place orders, access seller accounts, or use Pinterest data to identify or profile other people.

Storage and sharing

The application is designed to run locally on the operator's computer. OAuth tokens, search settings, notes, and the local research library are stored in the local application environment. The application does not sell personal information or share it with advertisers, data brokers, or unrelated third parties. Results continue to link to their original Etsy, eBay, or Pinterest pages.

Data retention and deletion

The operator can delete saved results, notes, credentials, and the local database from the application environment at any time. Removing the application credentials or disconnecting Pinterest invalidates the local connection; platform records remain subject to the relevant platform's policies.

Third-party services

When a platform is connected, its API and OAuth service process requests under that platform's own terms and privacy policy. Aivenor is an independent application and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Pinterest, Etsy, or eBay.

Contact

For questions about this policy or a request to remove information stored by the application, contact the application operator through the account or repository associated with Aivenor.

This policy applies to the Aivenor research application and its local data store. It does not replace the privacy policy of Pinterest, Etsy, eBay, GitHub, or any other platform the operator may visit.
